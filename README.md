One line, at most 200 characters: the museum label a museum in the year 3000 puts beside an ordinary object from today — an entry for make.fun's Museum of the Year 3000 bounty ($2, top 3 paid).

## Entry

> Group chat, c. 2026. A ritual chant performed nightly by up to 47 people, none of whom ever answered the original question.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) reads the line from `entry.txt` and writes it to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
